02 / About

Research with a practical edge.

I’m interested in the point where evidence meets judgement: who leads, how boards work, and what the data can tell us.

I spent five years in Russell Reynolds Associates’ Board and CEO Advisory practice, building research and analytical products for senior advisers. I created the firm’s Global CEO Turnover Index and worked across succession, board composition and governance.

My PhD examined knowledge systems and research productivity. After an INSEAD MBA, I’ve continued governance research with the INSEAD Centre for Corporate Governance while building independent tools and prototypes.
